# E003 calm: QR code parking scam (older viewers)
You scan the *code*
to pay for *parking*.
The page asks for
your *card number*.
Then you look back
at the *meter*
and notice a *corner*
*peeling up*.
|There's *another* QR code
*underneath*.
That *top sticker*
can send you
to a *fake* payment page.
Type in your *card number*
and the *scammer*
can get your *details*
while your parking
is still *unpaid*.
|If the code looks
*covered up*,
*close* the page.
Look up the *city*
or parking *operator's*
*official* payment app
or *website* yourself.
|Before you *pay*,
check where that *code*
is taking you.
